How to play

Classic 3×3 tic-tac-toe for two people on the same device

Goal

Be the first to place three of your marks in a row — across, down, or diagonally. If every square is filled with no winner, the game is a tie.

Turns

X always moves first. Tap an empty square to play, then pass the device to your opponent for O. The status line shows whose turn it is.

Board numbering

Each cell matches the classic number pad layout used in the terminal version:

1 | 2 | 3
--+--+--
4 | 5 | 6
--+--+--
7 | 8 | 9
